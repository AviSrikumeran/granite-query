# Evidence constellation: clarity revision

3 November 2026, America/Chicago. The owner said the constellation was confusing and asked to make its meaning clear. This request authorizes revising that study in place; the homepage and all other studies remain protected.

Live study: [Evidence constellation](https://avisrikumeran.github.io/granite-query/playground/constellation/). Compare with the [initial construction review](2026-11-03-constellation.md) and the preserved [results branches](https://avisrikumeran.github.io/granite-query/playground/results-branches/).

## What was unclear

Automatic Archive / Scatter / Trace / Resolve choreography made existing records look like new events or separate results. Abstract labels did not explain why the objects were connected. The visual had no clear starting point for someone trying to understand the study.

The intended meaning is simple: three source records support five checks, which inform one study conclusion. The moving pieces represent evidence already published. Their motion is a presentation, not a running query, live training or private model reasoning.

## Revision

- The first view is the organized conclusion pose, paused. The actual question and observed result appear above it.
- Source labels are Setup, Scores and Run record. Five named checks have matching numbers in a key; the bottom cube is labeled Conclusion.
- Selecting a tile explains its role in the scene and shows the validated evidence text and source links below. The record picker offers the same access by keyboard and on a narrow screen.
- Play animation opts into the existing sequence. Read the map restores the organized pose. Held scenes, restart and the scrubber are grouped under Animation options.
- Read selected evidence exits an expanded view before scrolling to the detail, so the target is visible.

The magenta material, geometry and expressive sequence remain. The data contract and source feed are unchanged. Counts and conclusions still derive from the public study, including uncertainty, pending and unavailable states.
