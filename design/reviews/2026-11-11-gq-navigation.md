# GQ navigation identity

The owner selected the Slow turn GQ treatment and explicitly chose navigation placement. The accepted mark has a complete G top stroke, distinct larger glyphs, a bright magenta material and a traveling light. All letter parts stay together; the original homepage sculpture remains in place.

## Integration

- Shared navigation module on Home, Results, Research/History, Try Granite and the standalone reading-page shell.
- One compact transparent WebGL canvas per page, with a dark backing on light headers. The full-size artwork's seven-degree rigid turn and traveling light are retained.
- Accessible home-link name, Space pause/resume and Escape pause. Reduced motion starts still. Hidden/offscreen rendering stops, and a plain GQ remains if graphics are unavailable or lost.
- The renderer derives from the preserved original material pipeline; the prior sculpture, study modules, research text and data remain unchanged.

## Validation

The 25 existing site tests passed. Browser checks confirmed the navigation canvas renders, the original homepage artwork still identifies as electric-magenta, Space holds its current phase, reduced motion starts paused, and missing WebGL leaves a visible accessible home link. The mobile menu works at 390 px; 390 px and 320 px homepage checks showed no document overflow. Dark and light headers were inspected. No model generation or training was run.

The glyphs read primarily as texture at navigation size. The detailed large-format mark remains the design reference; this release does not replace the homepage sculpture or redesign the research pages.
