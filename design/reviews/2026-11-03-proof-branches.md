## Visual and information design

The accepted [proof-branch direction](https://avisrikumeran.github.io/granite-query/playground/directions/) supplies the structural reference. The new graph has eleven nodes and fourteen paths: query, retrieval junction, three source records, five requirements and one answer. All five requirements contribute to the answer. Source associations remain explicit in both node details and the text walkthrough.

The example asks what would verify a reported gain. Its records and passages are illustrative requirements, not real research artifacts or model results. There are no fabricated scores or assertions of improvement. The graph describes an inspectable evidence route, without implying access to private model reasoning.

The new folder independently reuses the established electric magenta ramp, glyph atlas, lighting, ambient occlusion and bloom. Branches grow before their destination nodes appear. Scroll progress eases toward the reader's position; reversing scroll uses the same graph state. Selected nodes and adjacent paths brighten. Pointer movement gently turns both geometry and its accessible node controls together.

One sticky diagram accompanies four editorial sections. The complete text remains readable without JavaScript. Mobile uses the same diagram above the scrolling sections, with compact details and stage navigation. Labels sit beside the nodes so they do not cover the short vertical connectors. The mobile text begins near the top of each section to avoid an empty reading area below the sticky map.

## Verification

All new module syntax checks and six automated tests pass. They cover source ownership and contribution to the answer, parent-before-path timing across 501 samples, 251 samples of finite normalized path geometry, complete oriented-path endpoint attachment, scroll anchoring and reversible interpolation, node-control projection at mouse extremes on five view sizes, and preservation of the established magenta color ramp.

Browser checks cover normal scroll growth, reverse scrolling from eleven to five visible nodes, stage navigation, node detail selection including unresolved uncertainty, Show full map and Follow scroll, keyboard turn/reset, pointer turning at both edges, and WebGL and Canvas 2D rendering. The mobile layout has equal 375-pixel document and scroll widths with a 335 by 280 artwork view. The selected-node panel and navigation remain visible above the reading area. No warning or error was observed in either rendering path.

Reduced-motion preference handling, hidden/offscreen suspension and context recovery are source-reviewed. OS reduced motion and GPU context loss were not simulated. Reduced motion shows the complete map immediately, without scroll tweening or pointer follow; keyboard turning applies directly. Once input settles there is no autonomous render loop. No field performance or usability claim is made.

## Boundaries and next feedback

This is a presentation prototype. It does not query the training system or verify any reported result. Replace the illustrative records with traceable public artifacts before presenting the graph as real project evidence. Preserve missing checks, uncertainty and inconclusive findings in that integration.

The next useful feedback is whether the reader can follow which source owns each requirement, whether the scroll pacing feels natural, and whether the complete graph remains readable. The glyph treatment and compact node labels may need a different density when real records are longer. Keep this evidence map separate from the core-extraction hero.

Publication is limited to the new specimen, its test and review, design notes and the experiment catalogue. The homepage, earlier studies, training code and research outputs remain protected. All remote blobs outside the explicit whitelist are compared before the branch update.
