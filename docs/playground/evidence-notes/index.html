Field note / 001Research communication

Make every finding
inspectable.

A concise result should invite a closer look. The evidence can stay beside the sentence, without taking over the page.

Four habits of a readable research note↓
01

Start with the comparison.

Say what changed and what stayed fixed. A reader should be able to identify the alternative before interpreting a result.

A short note can carry this context in one sentence, while the full setup stays available in the margin.

02

Keep the unit visible.

The unit might be questions, examples or runs. It should travel with the number instead of being left in a distant method section.

03

Show the range, too.

An interval adds context to the point estimate. Selecting the sentence should reveal how that interval was obtained and what it supports.

The interface should give a careful result as much visual weight as an exciting one.

04

Keep the boundary attached.

Name what the evidence can establish and what remains open. The qualification is part of the finding.
