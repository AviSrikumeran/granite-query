What we learned

The larger adapter corrected all8 taught mistakes, up from0/8. It preserved8/8 replay answers but lost accuracy on separate questions. This is fitting without demonstrated transfer.

Only8 distinct mistake corrections plus8 replay examples were taught per arm. Increasing capacity, updates and learning rate together does not isolate a cause. Complete targets/EOS and checkpoint reloads were verified. Teacher correction SFT is not token-distribution-matching distillation.
