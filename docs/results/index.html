How to read these results
Development
Questions kept separate from training and used to compare the tested recipes. These adaptive samples are not a final test.
Same-task retention
Other questions from the same task, used to check what training retained. This does not measure broad ability.
Percentage points
The difference between two accuracy percentages. The named reference can change with the selected model.
Question-bootstrap interval
Uncertainty from resampling the evaluated questions. It does not measure variation between training seeds.

Interpretation / Keep the boundary
What the evidence does—and doesn’t—say.
The tested recipes failed to establish improvement. That is a result about these experiments, not proof that distillation or data selection cannot work.


Separate studies stay separate.
The 32-question correction study, 200-question matched comparison and earlier 64-question selection study use different cohorts and protocols. Their scores are not pooled.


Numeric agreement is not verified reasoning.
Answers were scored against numeric references. Reference-sensitive interpretations, formatting and truncation matter; correct final numbers do not validate every reasoning step.


Development evidence is not a final test.
These TRAIN-development samples were studied adaptively. One seed and question-bootstrap intervals do not establish seed-level reproducibility or broad generalization.


Inference settings are not training gains.
Untouched Granite scored 138/200 with OPEN thinking and a 1,024-token cap, and 175/200 with a 2,048-token cap and batch 32. These diagnostics changed inference settings; no trained checkpoint improvement follows. Read OPEN / 1,024 ↗ · Read OPEN / 2,048 ↗


More fitting changed several things at once.
Capacity, updates and learning rate changed together in the larger run. Training-token exposure also differed between arms. The outcome does not isolate a single causal explanation.
